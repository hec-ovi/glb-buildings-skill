# Sizes that look right

Metres, for the command line. These are what real buildings use, not code minima: a minimum produces the
cheapest legal box, not a building anyone would believe.

## Floor to floor

| Building | Ground floor | Upper floors |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Residential | 3.5 | 2.8 to 3.0 |
| Office | 4.5 | 4.0 (3.6 to 4.2) |
| Retail at the bottom | 4.3 to 4.5 | follows whatever sits above |
| Warehouse, single volume | 7.5 to 9.5 clear | one section |

Offices are about a metre taller per floor than flats because of the plenum: a raised floor plus ducts under
the beams adds 1.1 to 1.2 m of floor zone. Flats have no plenum.

## The crown

A parapet stands 1.0 to 1.2 m above the roof deck. Anything you can walk on needs it. A plant enclosure on top
is up to 5.5 m and looks like a small extra band.

## Footprint and bays

| Building | Footprint | Bay |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Apartment block | 15 x 12 to 25 x 18 | 3.0 |
| Office tower | 30 x 30 to 45 x 45 | 6.0 to 9.0 structural, 1.5 facade module |
| Narrow infill | 8 x 15 | 3.0 |

Floor plates are as deep as daylight reaches: about 2.5 times the window head height from the facade, so a
double-loaded plate lands near 14 m deep. Wider than that and the middle is dark.

## Heights that mean something

- 22.9 m to the top occupied floor is where a building becomes a high rise. About 8 residential floors, or 6
  office floors.
- Low rise reads as up to 4 floors, mid rise 5 to 10, high rise above that. These are conventions, not rules.

## Parts

Doors 0.9 x 2.1 clear. Window sill 0.75 to 0.9 above the floor, head 2.1 to 2.4 residential and 2.7 to 2.9
office. Balcony depth 2.0 minimum to be usable, 1.5 to 2.4 typical, guard rail 1.05 to 1.1 high with gaps under
0.1 so a child cannot pass. Stairs rise 0.17 to 0.18 with a 0.28 tread. Corridors 1.1 wide, 1.5 where two
wheelchairs pass. An air conditioning unit on a facade is roughly 0.55 high, 0.77 wide, 0.29 deep.

## Regions disagree

Guard rails are 1.07 in the US, 0.9 inside and 1.1 outside in the UK, 0.9 up to a 12 m drop and 1.1 above in
Germany, 0.95 in Chile. Stair risers max out at 0.178 (US), 0.22 (UK private), 0.19 (Germany), 0.18 (Mexico
City, Chile). When the user names a country, follow that column; otherwise use the middle of the range and say
which you used.
